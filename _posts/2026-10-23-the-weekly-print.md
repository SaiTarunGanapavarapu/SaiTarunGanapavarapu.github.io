## 4. Quant Research Digest

Three papers I found worth reading this week:

>**Lineage-Aware Search Control for LLM-Based Evolutionary Factor Mining** — Xintong Liu et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29663v1)  

This framework introduces a search control mechanism that actively tracks the genetic lineage of symbolic alpha factors generated by LLMs acting as evolutionary operators. By forcing the algorithm to recognize semantic ancestry, it prevents the LLM from getting trapped in local optima and generating redundant math variations, solving a major hurdle in automated factor discovery pipelines.

>**Dynamics of the Liquidity Profile in Concentrated Liquidity Provision: An SPIDE Approach** — Cheuk Yin Lee et | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30539v1)  

The authors model the aggregate liquidity profile in concentrated automated market makers (like Uniswap V3) using a stochastic partial integro-differential equation to capture provider repositioning. It offers a rigorous mathematical foundation for how liquidity clusters and diffuses around the active price, which is critical for backtesting execution algorithms in DeFi liquidity pools.

>**Equilibria and Learning in Competitive Market Making** — Matteo Onger et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30891v1)  

This paper formulates a competitive market-making game in a continuous quote space among strategic agents, addressing the non-convexities that usually break standard continuous existence proofs. It directly tackles the instability and convergence issues found when training multi-agent reinforcement learning models in order-driven limit order books.
